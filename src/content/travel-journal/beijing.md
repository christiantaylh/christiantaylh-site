  - type: text
    body: '14/10/19 - I was set to move to Australia, but I was yet to book my flight. I could fly YYZ to Sydney direct for $1,200 or fly out of Detroit with a 4-day layover in Beijing for $800... I love a good layover so obviously I went with the latter. I had never been to China, and I knew I could get a 144-hour transit visa on arrival.'
  - type: text
    body: 'I slept through the entire 13-hour flight, so after saying bye to my parents at the airport, the next thing I remember was waking up as the plane touched down in Beijing. But when I tried to get through immigration, they wouldn''t let me out of the airport. I don''t speak a lick of Mandarin, so this part is all broken translation between the border agent, the sympathetic Delta flight attendant, and myself, but apparently my "onward ticket to Hong Kong didn''t qualify me for the transit exemption because, according to the rules, my next destination couldn''t be another Chinese territory or region."'
  - type: image
    src: /uploads/travel-journal/beijing/flight.avif
    alt: Flight to Beijing
    width: full
  - type: text
    body: |-
      I'm dumb, but not dumb enough to say what I wanted to say: "But Hong Kong isn't China."

      The flight attendant looked at me and said, "You need to book another flight out of China right now, or they're going to send you back to the United States."

      Google was blocked, my phone barely worked, and I had no idea what to do. Seeing how lost I was, she handed me her own phone opened to a Chinese booking website. So I bought the cheapest flight out of mainland China so they would stamp my passport with the visa, and the next thing I knew I was smoking darts with my cab driver on my way to my hostel in Tiananmen Square.
