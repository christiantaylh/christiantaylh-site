  Ten years ago, Hagai Blonder walked out of architecture school. He moved to Canada, set up a shop in Victoria, BC, and started shaping surfboards by hand.

  His brief is unusual for the craft: every board he builds has to be beautiful, durable, and as close to 100% sustainable as the materials allow. The Art of Shaping follows him through one board, start to finish.
